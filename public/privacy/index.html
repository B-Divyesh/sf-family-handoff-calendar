Privacy, in plain language

Last updated: August 28, 2026. Family Handoff Calendar is designed so your family schedule stays on your device.

What is stored

Household member labels, events, assignments, locations, notes, and preferences are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. The app has no account system, cloud calendar, analytics tracker, advertising, location access, or child profile.

When data leaves

Your calendar leaves the browser only when you explicitly download an ICS or JSON file and choose where to send it. Treat exports as private: they can contain schedule and location details.

Licenses and payment

If you buy or restore a household pass, the license token is stored in localStorage and sent to the Sociobot billing API for verification at most once per day. Checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record. This app does not receive your card details.

Erase and control

Use “Erase this calendar” under Household, or clear this site’s browser data. Removing the app alone may not clear browser storage. Export a backup first if you need one.

Questions

Contact privacy@sociobot.in. Please do not email sensitive family schedule details.
